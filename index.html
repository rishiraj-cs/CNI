Online Appendix

Taxonomy of CNI Disruptions

Disruptions across various CNIs like Electricity, Water, Food Supply, Healthcare, Finance, etc.

This appendix generalises Figure 1 of the article. For each quadrant of the taxonomy, it reproduces the underlying case register — sector, incident type, date, description, and news links and academic references — as compiled for this study.

Source URLs are reproduced in full so that readers may verify provenance directly. Entries are added on a rolling basis as further quadrants are documented.

Sources are reproduced as originally reported. Inclusion here does not constitute independent verification of each claim; disputed allegations are noted as such in the case description where applicable. Academic citations are listed as supplied by the compiling researcher and should be checked against the original publication before further citation.
